      We've All Been There.

      Students we spoke with described these patterns in their own group assignments.

      Unequal Contributions

      A few people end up carrying the project while everyone shares the credit.

      Endless Follow-ups

      Reminders in the group chat get ignored, so someone ends up chasing everyone.

      Missed Deadlines

      One late part holds up the whole submission, and others scramble to cover it.

      Lack of Accountability

      When nobody clearly owns a task, nobody feels responsible for finishing it.

      Meet Grouply.

      Grouply is a concept for an academic coordination platform. It aims to make group assignments easier by putting responsibility, progress and deadlines in one shared place, so students spend less time chasing and more time creating.

      • Individual task ownership

        Every task has one named owner, so it's clear who is responsible for what.

      • Transparent progress tracking

        The whole group can see what's done and what's still pending, without asking.

      • Deadline management

        Personal due dates keep each part moving ahead of the final submission.

      Today, in many groups

      • Tasks agreed in a chat and easy to forget
      • Progress known only when you ask
      • Reminders sent one person at a time
      • Hard to see who did what

      With Grouply Proposed

      • Each task has an owner and a due date
      • Progress visible to everyone
      • Pending work is easy to spot
      • A record of contributions

      Where we are

      Grouply began as a simple task manager. Feedback showed that splitting tasks and setting deadlines doesn't fix unequal contributions on its own, so the idea now centres on accountability. It is an early-stage student startup concept: we have spoken with students to understand the problem, and the prototype is still being developed.

      How It Works

      Proposed workflow This is how we plan for Grouply to work once the prototype is built.

      1. 1

        Create your group

        Start a project and add the people you're working with.

      2. 2

        Assign tasks and deadlines

        Give each task an owner and a due date everyone can see.

      3. 3

        Track progress and complete your project together

        Follow what's done, spot what's pending and finish on time.
